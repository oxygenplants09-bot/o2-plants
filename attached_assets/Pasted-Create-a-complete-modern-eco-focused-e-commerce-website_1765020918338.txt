Create a complete, modern, eco-focused e-commerce website for a brand named Oxygen, a company dedicated to fighting pollution by providing air-purifying, anti-pollution indoor plants. The website should feel natural, clean, and mission-driven—reflecting our belief that healthier homes start with cleaner air.

Brand Essence

Oxygen is a sustainable plant brand that promotes pollution-free living through air-purifying plants. The design should communicate freshness, breathability, environmental responsibility, and calm. The site should instantly convey that we help people breathe cleaner air through nature.

Design Requirements

Use the following brand colors throughout the theme:

Background color: #17201b

Text color: #5ac65e

Call-to-action (button) color: #f5f5dc

The overall vibe should be:

Clean and minimal yet expressive

Nature-driven

Soft shadows and smooth rounded shapes

Organic and modern

Airy and refreshing

Use plenty of spacing, soft transitions, and a light, breathable visual feeling.

Website Structure

Create the following pages/sections:

1. Home Page

Hero section with a bold mission statement like:
“Fight Pollution, Breathe Better — Bring Home Anti-Pollution Plants.”

Background visuals of indoor plants with soft gradients.

A featured product grid (air-purifying plants).

Section explaining how plants remove toxins (simple infographic style).

“Why Oxygen?” section highlighting:

Air purification

Sustainable sourcing

Eco-friendly pots

Cleaner indoor environments

Testimonials section.

Call-to-action: “Shop Air-Purifying Plants” using CTA color.

2. Shop / Products Page

A clean grid of plant products.

Hover effects showing brief benefits (e.g., “Removes toxins,” “Improves air quality”).

Filters: price, light requirement, size, purification strength.

Product cards following brand colors.

3. Product Detail Page

Each product page should include:

Large plant image

Description focusing on air-purifying benefits

Care instructions

Pollution-reduction level

“Perfect for homes, offices, and pollution-heavy environments"

Add to cart button in CTA color

4. About Us Page

Tell the story of Oxygen, highlighting:

Our mission to fight pollution using nature

Commitment to sustainability

The environmental impact of keeping indoor plants

How Oxygen plants help create toxin-free indoor spaces

5. Contact Page

Clean layout

Simple contact form

Social links

Address & email

6. Navigation & Footer

Sticky header with brand logo and links

Footer with:

Quick links

Social icons

Small sustainability statement:
“Every plant you bring home helps clean the air we breathe.”

Tone & Copywriting Style

Friendly, calming, natural, and inspiring

Focus on environmental awareness & health

Clear, purposeful copy

Functionality

Responsive design

Add-to-cart & checkout flow

Search bar

Product category filters

Smooth animations

Clean product management layout

Overall Goal

Make the website feel like a breath of fresh air, where customers understand instantly that Oxygen stands for clean air, anti-pollution, sustainability, and nature-powered wellness.
